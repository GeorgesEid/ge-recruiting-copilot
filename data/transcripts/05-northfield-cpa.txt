Teams meeting transcript - Northfield CPA Group (Columbus, OH) / GE Staffing - Oct 15, 2026
Gary Lindqvist (Managing Partner), Beth (GE recruiter)

Gary: We're a 7-person CPA firm. Tax season kills us every year and the bookkeeping clients suffer. We want a remote bookkeeper, full time, year round.

Beth: What software?

Gary: QuickBooks Online mostly, about 80 percent of our clients. The rest are on Xero. So QuickBooks Online is a must, three years minimum, and Xero would be really nice. Both is the dream.

Beth: What kind of clients?

Gary: US small businesses. Restaurants, contractors, a couple of dental offices, some e-commerce. They need to know US stuff - sales tax basics, 1099 contractors, how US banks and credit cards show up in the feeds.

Beth: Tasks?

Gary: Bank and credit card reconciliations, categorizing transactions, month-end close, AP with Bill.com for some clients, payroll entries from Gusto. We do the tax returns, they don't need to do taxes.

Beth: Certifications?

Gary: QuickBooks ProAdvisor certification is a plus. Not required.

Beth: Hours?

Gary: Eastern time, at least until 1pm Eastern. During tax season, February to April, we might ask for a few extra hours.

Beth: Budget?

Gary: 1,500 to 2,000 a month depending on experience.

Beth: Anything else?

Gary: Accuracy. If they reconcile something wrong and we file on it, that's my license. And they should ask questions instead of guessing. Oh - and they must be okay with our security rules: our laptop policy, 2FA everywhere, no client data on personal email.

Beth: Understood. I'll write the job description and send it to you for approval first.
